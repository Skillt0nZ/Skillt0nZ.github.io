SKILLT0NZ WEBSITE — DENJI ENERGY EDITION

INSTALL
1. Extract this entire folder somewhere on your computer.
2. Keep index.html, anime.html, photos.html, style.css, and JavaScript.js together.
3. Keep the Images and Photos folders beside those files; keep image paths exactly as referenced in the HTML files.
4. Double-click index.html to open the site in your browser.

WHAT'S NEW
- The original cream / black / red punk-zine theme is preserved.
- Chainsaw Man is clearly marked as Ferran's favourite anime, with a dedicated feature panel.
- The "PULL THE CORD" button triggers a Denji-style animation and a synthesized engine sound when sound effects are enabled.
- The "REV THE ENGINE" button also triggers the engine animation and sound.
- SFX starts OFF. Click SFX OFF in the navigation to enable sounds. Sounds are generated by the browser; no external audio files are required.
- 3D tilt interactions on the poster and anime/photo cards (desktop pointer devices).
- Improved text contrast and clearer supporting text.
- Theme button switches between paper and dark themes.
- Menu button opens navigation on narrow screens.
- Anime search and genre filters work together.
- PICK FOR ME chooses a random anime from the visible results and opens its details.
- Clicking an anime poster opens a details modal. Escape or the close button dismisses it.
- Photo gallery placeholders are intentional until you add your own photos.

ADDING PHOTOS
Put photos in the Photos folder and name them photo-1.jpg, photo-2.jpg, photo-3.jpg,
photo-4.jpg, photo-5.jpg, and photo-6.jpg. The gallery reveals each image once present.

The theme choice is remembered by your browser when local storage is available.
Sound effects are opt-in and are not played automatically.
